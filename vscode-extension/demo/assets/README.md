Viaalink brand assets for the demo film. The SVGs are copied from the viaalink-website repo (`static/assets/logo-wordmark.svg` and `logo-wordmark-dark.svg`). The PNGs are rendered from them with transparency:

- `viaalink-wordmark.png`: red mark and navy letters, for the white title and end cards.
  `inkscape viaalink-wordmark.svg --export-type=png --export-width=2400 --export-filename=viaalink-wordmark.png`
- `viaalink-wordmark-dark.png`: red mark and light letters, for the corner of the dark film.
  `inkscape viaalink-wordmark-dark.svg --export-type=png --export-width=1200 --export-filename=viaalink-wordmark-dark.png`
